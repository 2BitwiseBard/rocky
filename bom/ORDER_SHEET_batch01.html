⚠ The three traps

  • 12 V variant only. ST3215 listings mix the 7.4 V (19.5 kg·cm) and 12 V (30 kg·cm) variants. Only 12 V closes Pebble's torque budget. Confirm "30KG" and 12 V before checkout (Feetech SKU ST-3215-C018/C047).
  • ST3235 is not a 35 kg·cm servo. Despite the name it's 30 kg·cm — same torque as ST3215, aluminum shell, $49.99. A durability upgrade, not a power upgrade. Skip it.
  • SCS0009 is a 6 V device. It must NOT sit on the 12 V servo power rail (it shares the TTL data bus, but its V+ comes from a 5–6 V rail — bench PSU at 6 V for now, small BEC on the robot later). Master-plan wiring diagram gets amended (D016).
