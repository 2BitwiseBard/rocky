⚠ The traps, batch-3 edition

  • Vacuum-lidar pulls: buy LDS02RR, not Neato. Xiaomi/Roborock LDS02RR pulls are $15–17 with an actively maintained ROS2 driver (Maker's Pet, updated 2026-06). Neato XV-11 units have aged into ~$60 collector stock — skip.
  • Ultrasonics: US-100, not HC-SR04. The Pi is 3.3 V; HC-SR04 echoes at 5 V (level shifting per sensor). US-100 is 3.3 V native with a UART mode + temp compensation. Worth the extra dollars ×5.
  • ReSpeaker: v2.0 only. The v1 (WM8960) is retired; Seeed's v2.0 (TLV320AIC3104, $13.99) is the buyable one with working kernel-6.x drivers.
  • Genuine Amass lives at RC-battery shops. XT30/XT60 pairs cost ~¼ of the Amazon "Amass" multipacks. Same order as the UBEC.
