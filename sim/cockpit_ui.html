Hardware (real servos)

      
The Feetech bus beside the sim. Connect the adapter, scan, and every leg whose three servos answered becomes a real leg: sim → robot streams the sim's joint targets to it (gait, gestures, reflexes), robot → sim makes the sim leg follow the real one (hold it in your hand; the sim shows what the software thinks the pose is). Legs not on the bus stay simulated. mock is the byte-level servo simulator the bench scripts rehearse on.
