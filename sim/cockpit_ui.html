World & map?

        

        
Top-down map: robot (purple, heading tick), objects from the world spec (grey), lidar hits (blue dots), goto target (cyan), a latched void (red wedge), remembered things (pink rings, see Memory below: dashed = vague, grey = stale). Click the map to send a goto there; click a pink ring to go back to it or forget it; right-click or long-press to pin a name there. The lidar puck sees only what stands above its plane (~0.18 m): low boxes and rubble end a goto as 'stuck', tall ones as 'blocked'.

Robot

      
Hardware (real servos)?

        
The Feetech bus beside the sim. Connect the adapter, scan, and every leg whose three servos answered becomes a real leg: sim → robot streams the sim's joint targets to it (gestures, reflexes; soft entry: parked where it is, 40 % torque, 200 c/s, then released), robot → sim makes the sim leg follow the real one. sim → robot needs the sim at a planted standstill, and holds locomotion until the real feet report contacts. mock is the byte-level servo simulator the bench scripts rehearse on.
