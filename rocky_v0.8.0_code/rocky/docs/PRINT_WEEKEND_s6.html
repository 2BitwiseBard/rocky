Print Weekend — session 6 plan

Everything below prints from cad/out/*.stl in the v0.7.4 repo (session-6 build — fit ladder v2 and the tibia clamp fix are in). All plates are PLA (D011: dial the machine on the cheap spool; the purple PETG stays in the drawer until parts stop changing).
Before the printer warms up: place the Batch 0+1 carts. The AliExpress/Waveshare servo cart is a 1–4 week lead and gates the entire bench phase — calibration, the caliper session, params v1, first real walking. A perfect print weekend without that order is plastic waiting on a boat. bom/ORDER_SHEET_batch01.html has every cart linked; the servo cart is the only line that can stall the project a month.

0 · Machine prep (textured sheet, PLA)

  • Sheet: the textured PEI you own is the right sheet for this project — PETG releases from it without glue and PLA holds fine a touch warmer. A smooth sheet is a someday nice-to-have, not a blocker.
  • Wash the sheet: dish soap + hot water, dry with a paper towel. (IPA alone smears finger oils around textured plates.)
  • PLA on textured: nozzle 210–215 °C, bed 60 °C first layer / 60–65 °C rest. If corners of the fit ladder lift, go 65 °C + a 3 mm brim.
  • Re-run Live-Z on THIS sheet — textured needs a noticeably lower (more negative) offset than smooth; your calibration from the z-tune may not carry over. Textured plates forgive slight over-squish; err a hair closer.
  • Slicer: 0.2 mm layers, elephant-foot compensation 0.15 mm ON (first-layer squish eats bore diameters otherwise), seam = rear/aligned.

4 · Sunday — dry-fit: what "body + one leg together" means with zero hardware

  • Works now: the port dance on the REAL deck × coxa base (all five stations); hand fully assembles (filament pins + cam) and bayonets onto the slider; tools swap on the same socket; couplers seat in the recess demo; latches click into the deck strikes; dovetail shoes slide the sector bars; two sectors interlock at the seam.
  • New this session — servo_blank: a printed, dimensionally exact ST3215 stand-in (boolean-proven to drop into all three cradles; horn disc takes real M2 screws). With 3 blanks the WHOLE leg chain assembles rigid: blank in the coxa cradle, fork on its horn, blank in the fork rails, femur_link screwed to its horn disc, blank in the knee carrier — joints frozen at print angle, every interface real. That is your "body + one leg together" this weekend.
  • Still waits for hardware: tube + spring + switch = the shin below the carrier; bearings = the crown steady; inserts + thumbscrews = the port's final clamp; and nothing MOVES until real servos land. This weekend buys verified interfaces and dialed tolerances, so servo-arrival day is pure assembly.
  • Failed prints go in the graveyard box and get a line in NOTES_INBOX — failures are first-class data.
