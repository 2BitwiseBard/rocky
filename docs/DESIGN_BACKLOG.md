## Session-9f additions (2026-09-24 review, D052)

| # | idea | verdict | evidence / where |
|---|---|---|---|
| B33 | CAD-derived CoM / inertia, an SEA slide joint, ToF floor sensors | **PROPOSED** | Three things the D052 model still guesses. (a) Masses come from CAD volumes, but each link's CoM and inertia are primitive stand-ins (torso = two cylinders split 0.75 / 0.25, shin CoM at 0.46·L3); build123d gives each part's centroid and inertia tensor, so `mass_audit` could write them and `build_mjcf` / `generate_urdf` / the feasibility CoM model (which mirrors `build_mjcf.leg_xml`) read them. Matters most for `manip_adjacent`, whose CoM margin is 17.5 mm. (b) The SEA cartridge (D010: 7 mm travel) is rigid in the sim: a slide joint with the spring rate would make landings, shoves and the foot switch (which closes on spring compression) physical, instead of a force threshold on a rigid sphere. (c) A downward ToF sensor per leg (or on the shell) would see a void *before* a foot probes into it — the lidar plane (~0.18 m) is blind below, and the probe-based void guard, even with the D052 touchdown gate, still falls in a lip band of approach angles (7 of 310 on a 1° grid, D052a): a foot centred 0–9 mm past the edge sits on the corner, closes its switch and slides off — nothing a foot switch can see, which a look-ahead sensor would. (a) is software only; (b) needs the spring rate from the bench; (c) needs a part choice, a mount and pins on the harness. |
| B34 | Overnight righter retrain on the D052 contract | **NEXT** (recipe ready, not run) | Train on the amended model (fingerprint `ceb63a1254c3`: leg forcerange = stall 2.94 N·m, the continuous budget as `rl_common.ThermalProxy`), not the `5a32…` 1.911 clip.
