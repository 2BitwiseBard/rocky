ST3215s close every case, but the knees run warm doing the coolest new trick. These are holding torques — dynamic peaks ride on top, and you said you'd rather have headroom.

Recommended buy (default-checked below): 10× ST3215 (yaws + hips) + 5× STS3250 (knees) + 1 ST3215 spare. Budget alt: all-ST3215 (fine, knees warm). Max: all-STS3250 (~$300 more, buys margin everywhere but the audit doesn't demand it). Skip the ST3215-HS "high speed" — it's a 20 kg·cm torque downgrade.

⚠ The traps (unchanged + one new)

  • 12 V variant only. ST3215 listings still mix 7.4 V (19.5 kg·cm) and 12 V (30 kg·cm). Confirm "30KG" and 12 V at checkout.
  • SCS0009 is a 6 V device — never on the 12 V rail (D016). Shares the data bus only.
  • NEW: STS3250 eBay listings hide the price in variants. The "$17" headline on some listings is the URT-1 adapter board alone; the servo bundles are $50–75+ship. Read the variant dropdown before celebrating.
  • STS3250 bench caveats stand (D015): spec'd 6–12 V vs 12.6 V full 3S — verify on the bench PSU before it meets a battery; 4.2 A stall each, so budget the power bus for two simultaneous stalls.
